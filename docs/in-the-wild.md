- Every everyday scene, with the rare ones blurred: [the collection](https://token-tv.vercel.app/collection/)

Facts that are easy to get wrong:

- The clock keeps its stock firmware. TokenTV sends it pictures over Wi-Fi through its photo album page.
- A computer on the same network has to stay on; it reads your usage and draws the pictures.
- Claude Code reactions (bow, raised hand, trial, sulk) need the TokenTV plugin and work on macOS and Linux, not Windows. Codex bows at the end of a turn once its `notify` setting points at TokenTV. Usage limits show on every OS.
- The numbers in the clips are sample values, not live ones.
- Price: these clocks usually sell for about US$5–13 on AliExpress, depending on region, shipping and deals. The author's cost about US$4. Which clocks work: [hardware compatibility](hardware-compatibility.md).
- The secret scenes exist and can be collected; their conditions are not public.
